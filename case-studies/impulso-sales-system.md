# Impulso Sales System - CRM + AI Sales Automation

## Problem
Sales conversations, follow-ups, appointments and lead status can become fragmented across WhatsApp, calendars and manual notes.

## Solution
A sales operations system that centralizes pipeline management, conversation context, appointments, reporting and AI-assisted automation.

## What I worked on
- n8n workflows for lead processing and sales automation.
- JavaScript logic for deterministic routing and validation.
- SQL/Supabase data flows for contacts, conversations, opportunities and appointments.
- WhatsApp integration and message processing.
- Calendar scheduling flows.
- Sales dashboard with KPIs, pipeline, client records, conversations and reports.
- Error handling and production debugging based on real workflow executions.

## Stack
n8n, JavaScript, SQL, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Next.js, TypeScript, WhatsApp, Google Calendar, Railway and Vercel.

## Example engineering principle
The sales workflow separates language generation from business decisions. The model can write a natural response, while deterministic logic decides whether required fields are complete, whether a lead can advance and when scheduling tools may be called.

## Status
Commercial/private system. Sanitized architecture can be discussed during interviews.
