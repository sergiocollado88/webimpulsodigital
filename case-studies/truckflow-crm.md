# TruckFlow CRM - Fleet Operations Platform

## Problem
Small and growing trucking operations need one place to manage loads, drivers, vehicles, diesel, expenses, payroll, documents and financial visibility.

## Solution
A role-based operational CRM designed around trucking workflows and permissions.

## What I worked on
- Loads/routes workflow.
- Drivers, trucks and trailers.
- Diesel and truck-expense reporting and approval.
- Brokers, factoring and payroll logic.
- Document storage and signed-access workflows.
- Role-based permissions and row-level security.
- Operational and accounting-oriented reporting.

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Row Level Security and Storage.

## Engineering focus
The project emphasizes permission boundaries, traceable financial operations and separation of sensitive information by role.

## Status
Private product codebase. Architecture and workflow design can be discussed during interviews.
